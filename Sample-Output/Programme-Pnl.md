## Method

- Each row is converted at the monthly rate for the month of the transaction, rounded half up to a minor unit.
- Fees received are held as a liability and recognised per day across the programme's start and end dates, up to the as-of date.
- Overhead (code OVH) is allocated to programmes pro rata to recognised revenue, largest remainder, so the parts sum exactly.
- Margin is recognised revenue minus direct costs minus allocated overhead.
